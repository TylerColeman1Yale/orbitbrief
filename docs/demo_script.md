# 2–3 minute demo

Start `npm start` and open the printed URL. Rotate the Earth and click a marker. Select `DEBRIS`, enter 500 and 800 km, and show the count strip. Submit “How many cataloged objects are between 500 and 800 km? Show them.” Show the tool trace, evidence link, and banner. Click `+ 1 hour`, reset filters, ask about debris removal, then ask about the Iridium/Cosmos collision. Close by showing the snapshot status and explaining that the scene is educational propagation rather than collision assessment.
